NaN falls back to `min` for the raw clamp and zero for percentage; infinities, equal/reversed and nonfinite bounds retain the source arithmetic without validation. The private formatter cache uses the pinned locale/options key and formatting semantics; its omitted nullable input is unreachable through Meter's required numeric API. No shared range helper extraction or local behavioral correction is included.
