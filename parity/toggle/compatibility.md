Form/type/value stripping, initial controlled mode/default, callback cancellation and ordinary prop precedence are fidelity requirements, not intentional differences. Custom replacement snippets may explicitly add their own host props just as upstream render elements can; this does not make stripped component props forwardable.

The pinned Toggle onClick closes over rendered pressed state. Automatic review at `3304c8f` and independent actual React/Svelte reproductions exposed a local live-read defect: an earlier consumer click writing owner=true produced a local false request instead of upstream true; two uncontrolled clicks before commit produced local true/false instead of upstream true/true. Generating a snapshot closure inside `$derived.by` restores both pinned outcomes and refreshes it after render. Three local regressions failed before this repair and pass afterward; six paired browser supplements cover consumer/replacement writes, same-turn clicks and post-commit refresh. This is a fidelity repair with no new intentional timing adaptation, helper change or parity credit. See [verification](verification.md) and [automatic finding](https://github.com/sveltery/base/pull/27#discussion_r4164027854).

The parent authorized serialized public exports and central register/catalog additions after Separator main; [shared-integration.md](shared-integration.md) records the integrated bounded additions. Passing tests or landing does not establish approval of an unapproved adaptation.
