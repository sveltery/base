# Contained Dialog supplemental probe traceability

These tests are narrower, separately authored supplements. **They do not port or earn credit for any of the original 175 declarations / 371 records.** Linked scenario IDs identify related source requirements, not claims that every assertion, fixture variant or dependency ran. Original [scenario contracts](scenarios.md) and inventory remain unchanged. Browser probes live in [dialog.spec.ts](../../tests/browser/dialog.spec.ts) and [dialog-focus-ownership.spec.ts](../../tests/browser/dialog-focus-ownership.spec.ts); mounted synthetic-DOM regressions live in [dialog.test.ts](../../packages/base/tests/dom/dialog.test.ts) and [focus-ownership.test.ts](../../packages/base/tests/dom/focus-ownership.test.ts).

The focus ownership audit adds paired real React/Svelte fixtures for inactive contained Trigger focus/press, conditional Portal and Popup removal, simultaneous close/removal, ordinary close without duplicate final focus, false/undefined final-focus callback results, and named-radio Tab containment. Trusted browser probes also switch the checked radio with ArrowRight and verify both Tab directions, including no tabbable radios when the checked or first group owner has negative tabindex; DOM companions check dynamic checked state, independent form ownership and unnamed radios. The focus cases reference the pinned [FloatingFocusManager](https://github.com/mui/base-ui/blob/47b40521eab921c2756bf9bdb0b0f07fbfdb8c8c/packages/react/src/floating-ui-react/components/FloatingFocusManager.tsx) and [tabbable discovery](https://github.com/mui/base-ui/blob/47b40521eab921c2756bf9bdb0b0f07fbfdb8c8c/packages/react/src/floating-ui-react/utils/tabbable.ts) behavior, without translating a complete upstream leaf. Read exact-head [CI](https://github.com/sveltery/base/actions/workflows/ci.yml) logs for secured execution results. Full nonmodal portal Tab order, modal accessibility isolation and new components remain separate unported work.

Default/boolean final focus on conditional removal preserves focus already moved outside; explicit callbacks retain their requested target. Paired DOM and browser companions exercise default restoration, external focus preservation with pointer dismissal disabled, and the explicit callback override.
