## Native adapter and legacy consumers

The attachment adapter publishes the actual host and invokes the source merged-ref callback. Native pre-update cleanup handles ref/default-tag/supplied-snippet replacements before attribute mutation; owner removal and host replacement internal to an unchanged snippet retain the separately documented after-removal observation. No fabricated DOM, reconnects or private Svelte internals are used. Historical scoped PM decisions remain history and do not establish this source gate.

Legacy `dialog/Element.svelte` callers already supply their own attributes and therefore temporarily suppress generic auto attributes with an explicitly labeled mapping adapter. This is an intermediate convergence step only. Those consumers still require their own pinned mapping/dependency audit and are not structurally cleared by the common renderer change. The fresh Field.Control port uses its real `fieldValidityMapping` and ordered source parameters directly.

Final reviewed commit, exact used symbols, utility dependency commit, caller suppression ledger and executed gates will be recorded after implementation. Current status: implementation and independent source review pending.
