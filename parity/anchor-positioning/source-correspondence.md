Named barrel imports resolve the consumed symbols; namespace imports also traverse their namespace exports. Unused named reexports are not dependencies. Imports inside a reached module are included conservatively even when a particular imported function is not on the consumed path. [ledger.json](ledger.json) separately preserves original assertion bodies and exact source snapshots.

This foundation does not implement them. The native geometry driver is never described as a port of those stores or as a complete `FloatingContext`.
